## 1. Probability Theory

Let $X$ follow a standard normal distribution.

$$
X \sim \mathcal{N}(0,1)
$$

Its probability density function is:

$$
f(x) =
\frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}
e^{-x^2/2}
$$

## 2. Gradient Descent

Consider the loss function:

$$
L(\theta) =
\frac{1}{n}
\sum_{i=1}^{n}
(y_i-f_\theta(x_i))^2
$$

The gradient descent update is:

$$
\theta_{t+1}
=
\theta_t-\eta\nabla_\theta L(\theta_t)
$$
